## Arduino & Hardware Interface Configuration

When configuring an Arduino interface:
- **Sketch Compatibility**: Both **Race Coordinator AI sketches (`v2.1.0.x`)** and original **Race Coordinator 1.0 sketches (`v1.0.0.x`)** are fully supported.
- **Pin Assignments**: You can assign digital and analog pins to lap counting, track power relays, call buttons, segment timing, and analog fuel level sensing on any supported sketch.
- **RGB LEDs**: FastLED RGB LED lighting (start lights, pit lane status, leader indicators) requires the Race Coordinator AI sketch (`v2.1.0.x`). When connected to an Arduino with the legacy Race Coordinator 1.0 sketch, the RGB LED options are disabled with an informational tooltip.
